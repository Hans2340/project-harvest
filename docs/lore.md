# Lore: Hikayat Sungai Seri (v2, draft)

Status: ✅ main story kept by leoxent (2026-11-21): cast, Four Guardians, acts, three endings.
The structure (cinematic → prologue with Awang → acts → epilogue) is in `game-design-proposed.md`.
Parts still marked "proposed" here are not decided.
Builds on the approved parts of `game-design.md` (era, Ordo Sigillum, the legends, classes).

## Rules for the story (religion)
The game is set in 15th-century Melaka, when Islam was spreading upriver. The story must not
teach or glorify shirk:
- Unseen beings (jin, Bunian, hantu) are **creations of God** with their own world. They are
  never protectors, never prayed to, never asked for help.
- No offerings, jampi (spells) or azimat (amulets) shown as good. Pawang = healer/expert
  (herbs, signs of nature, animal behaviour), who may **talk** to beings but never commands them.
- Islam is part of village life (azan subuh, the surau, Tok Imam), shown with respect, never
  preachy and never used as magic. No Quran verses or Arabic writing in art.
- The player's powers are **Bakat** (talents from within), levelled by **good deeds**, not
  offerings. To do in `game-design.md` (listed in `game-design-proposed.md`, Part B): "offerings at shrines" → good deeds; "azimat" → ramuan
  (remedies); Mata Batin → Firasat; keramat → Batu Sempadan.
- **Two meanings of "pawang".** Tok Senik is a pawang in the old sense: a healer who may talk to
  beings. The **Pawang class** is now a ranged elemental mage whose power is an **Anugerah** used
  through a medium, never calling on beings (see `game-design-proposed.md`, S27, which also asks whether to
  rename it).
- **Anugerah** (the magic system): some people are born with a gift from God to use the power
  of nature (water, fire, wind, earth...). It is never bought, bargained for or given by beings.
  It is rare, and the Ordo hunt those who have it. Awang and Kelana have one; the player has one
  only if they choose the Pawang class (name KIV). **Bakat** is different: every class has one, a
  unique passive or active talent.

## Cast
- **The player**: a farmer's child from a kampung upriver on the Seri.
- **Awang**: the player's older brother, who raised and looked after them after their father
  died of an illness. Farmer, hunter and pesilat; the player's first mentor and companion. **Has an Anugerah**,
  kept secret from the village until the raid (element: **Air**, water). Taken to the mines at the
  end of the prologue.
- **Mak**: waits at the jetty every evening.
- **Tok Senik**: old healer, keeps a guilty secret (he taught Kelana).
- **Tok Imam Hasyim**: imam of the surau, gentle and unbending.
- **Kelana (Warden Kelanus)**: main villain. A local Malay, Tok Senik's former student. **Has an
  Anugerah** (element: **Bumi**, earth: the gift he turned into nailing and binding).
- **Severin**: young Ordo Acolyte, joined because he was starving; can defect.
- **Si Raya**: a hantu raya that does **not** want to be freed: the hidden mastermind.
- **The Four Guardians** (creatures, not gods): Naga Tasik Chini (lake), Bujang Senang (swamp
  and river mouth), Orang Bunian (forest), Puteri Gunung Ledang (mountain).

## World
- Long ago the Four Guardians agreed the borders between the human world and the unseen world.
  **Batu Sempadan** (boundary stones) mark them. They are not worshipped: they are a fence
  between neighbours.
- **The forest is part of the human world.** Hunting, felling trees, gathering herbs and mining
  are all allowed and are core gameplay. The border is not the forest edge: it is the line marked
  by the Batu Sempadan. Beyond it lies the unseen world (e.g. the hidden Bunian village), which
  people enter only when invited (as in Act 3), like a neighbour's house.
- **Adab hutan** (forest manners, an ethic rather than a superstition, proposed): do not hunt
  more than you need, do not kill mothers with young, do not fell the trees around a Batu
  Sempadan. Following them counts as good deeds; breaking them makes animals scarce. Pantang larang
  that depend on belief in spirits are never shown as true.
- **Ordo Sigillum** came on pale-sailed carracks from a land they had squeezed dry. Their belief:
  every living thing can be owned. They nail the boundary stones, drag beings out of their world
  and enslave them in iron lanterns. Bound beings are **victims**. Locals call the emblem
  "Si Mata Berpaku".
- **Binatang Berpaku** (approved by leoxent, 2026-11-21): ordinary animals poisoned by the "seal poison"
  leaking from nailed stones (wild boar, tiger, python, monitor lizard, monkeys, bats, hornets,
  young crocodiles). Daily enemies; fewer once a zone's stone is freed. Still proposed: empty
  armour moved by a bound being (break the lantern, the being goes free).

## Story

**Before the prologue (the opening cinematic).** The Ordo have landed at the Kuala and nailed the
first stones there; villages downriver have gone quiet. Upriver the news has not arrived yet.

**Prologue: life with Awang, and the night he was taken.** Peaceful days in the kampung: Awang
teaches the player to farm, sell, fight, hunt and gather (missions in `game-design-proposed.md`). Small
signs of trouble come upriver. Then the Ordo arrive for the first time: they take control, force a
harvest tax and seize crops and goods, led by a Malay man in a grey robe with a whispering lantern
("I am from here too. I do this to save you."). Awang refuses to bow and protects the villagers,
and to do it he uses the Anugerah he has always hidden. Kelana sees it ("Kau pun sama macam
aku.") and takes **Awang** to the mines. (If the player is Pawang, Awang secretly taught them
their first cast on day 2; otherwise this is the moment the player learns his secret.) The swamp's boundary stone is nailed and the sawah withers overnight.
Tok Senik: "His name is Kelana. I taught him."
**Personal goal: bring Awang home.**

**Act 1: Paya Bakau.** The kampung changes (neighbours leave, the surau empties, Mak waits at the
jetty). Survive: farm, hide harvest from tax collectors, fight Binatang Berpaku. Bujang Senang
rages with an iron nail in his jaw; pull it out. He knew Kelana as a crying boy twenty years ago,
whose village was destroyed by a hantu raya that Bujang Senang drove upriver: "the boy brought it
back." Reward: the swamp is free, Ordo ships keep out; **a tooth** (key piece 1).
Theme: Kelana is a victim who became a predator.

**Act 2: Tasik Chini.** The tin mine by the lake, which Ordo is draining. Meet **Severin**
(can be persuaded to defect and become an informant). Find Awang alive but changed: he is a mine
overseer who whips other prisoners to survive, and refuses to leave ("If I run, they kill ten
more."). The Naga is weak; the Naga and Bujang Senang have an old feud (lake vs river mouth).
Reconcile them with the tooth; the Naga floods the mine and the prisoners escape. Awang is lost
in the chaos. Reward: **Sisik Naga** (key piece 2).

**Act 3: Rimba Bunian.** Earn the Bunian's trust by helping, not asking. Tok Senik confesses: he
taught orphan Kelana the boundary lore; Kelana wanted to lock away every unseen being so no
village would suffer again; Tok Senik refused; Kelana took the lore to the Ordo. Tok Senik and Tok
Imam finally sit together (Imam: "Fear of creatures made him lose his way. True fear is for God
alone."). The Bunian reveal the whisper in Kelana's lantern is **Si Raya**, the hantu raya that
destroyed his village, steering him all along. Reward: **Daun Bunian** (key piece 3).

**Act 4: Gunung Ledang.** Puteri once tested Kelana with impossible demands; he tried to meet them
with cruelty and failed. Now the Grandmaster comes with an army. She gives the player the same
demands: each has a fast cruel way and a hard just way (she tests character, not objects). Awang
returns as Kelana's lantern bearer, whispered to by Si Raya. Defeat him, or wake him: depends on
earlier deeds (people saved, cruel choices). Reward: **Selendang Puteri** (key piece 4).

**Act 5: Kuala.** The white fort; the **Great Seal** (a giant eye held by a thousand nails, each
binding a being). Allies arrive: Bujang Senang from the water, the Naga's flood, the Bunian from
the shadows, Severin opening the back gate (if persuaded), the risen villagers. Facing Kelana, Si
Raya leaves the lantern and possesses the Great Seal itself. If Kelana has woken, he fights beside
the player. The four pieces pull the Seal; every bound being goes home; Si Raya is **driven back
across the border**, not killed (it is a creature with its own place, just not here).

**Endings.**
| Ending | Condition | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Pulang | Awang woken, village spirit high | Awang comes home, Mak cries at the jetty; Kelana surrenders and rebuilds the stones with Tok Senik. |
| Pahit | Awang fell | Ordo falls, the player returns alone and now waits at the jetty with Mak. |
| Tunduk | Always paid, rarely resisted | Village safe but Ordo stays at the Kuala; Severin smuggles out a map (a hint of hope). |
Epilogue, **Sungai Masih Mengalir**: the first dawn after victory, the azan from a full surau; Tok Senik and Tok Imam on the
surau steps watching the sun over green sawah. "Kezaliman tak pernah kekal. Dan sungai ini...
masih ingat siapa tuannya."

## Game flow (summary)
- Main menu → opening cinematic (`docs/opening-scene.md`) → Tok Senik's questions (class,
  Bakat, look) → prologue with Awang → Ordo arrive, Awang taken → Acts 1–5 → ending → epilogue
  → free play after the ending.
- Prologue (approved: 6 tutorial missions, 30–45 min, see `game-design-proposed.md`). Suggested layout in
  S25: 3 in-game days with scripted time skips; the first Binatang Berpaku at the end of the hunt
  on day 2; day 3 the Ordo's first tax day with the first choice (pay, hide, fight); that night
  the swamp stone is nailed and Awang is taken.
- Daily loop: subuh → farm → one of market / help villagers / explore / train → evening news at
  the surau → night danger (raids, patrols, stealth missions) → sleep.
- Weekly: Ordo tax day, with meters for Fear ↔ Spirit, NPC Trust, and Ordo Control per zone
  (Ordo zones look pale; freed zones regain colour).
- Seasons: one act ≈ one 28-day season; Tengkujuh floods paths and opens perahu routes.

## Co-op (summary)
2–4 players join the host's world (Stardew style). Shared farm, shared clock (new day when all
sleep), classes complement each other (Hulubalang tank, Pendekar combos, Bayang stealth, Pawang
ranged damage and crowd control; healing from ramuan, see S27), revive downed friends, vote on big choices (host breaks ties). Host-authoritative:
clients send intents, the host applies them (the player already sends `use_requested(tool, cell)`
to the zone). Same zone first, split zones later. Build solo first, keep code co-op-ready.
